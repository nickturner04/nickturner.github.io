Air Conditioning Unit

Hard Surface Modelling and Texturing

This was a personal project I undertook to practice Hard Surface Modelling using Subdivision Surfaces. The models were created in Blender and are very high poly, in the style of product visualisation. The textures were made in Substance Painter.
